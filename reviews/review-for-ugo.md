the cross-context, automatic, detrimental gating into insurance and housing-adjacent products is
what makes me read it as social scoring rather than a garden-variety insurance risk-scoring

damagers for their own deposit/insurance terms), I'd call that high-risk, approvable with
controls. It's the *unrelated-context reuse* that pushes it further, so that's the detail worth
confirming came through intentionally in your brief.
